Appearance, moved in-panel — design options

The Appearance editor currently lives in a separate 760×820 native window with its own chrome. These mockups explore ways to bring it into the tray panel using the same language as AccountManagementDialog — bg-ui-scrim overlay, centered rounded-2xl card on the menu surface, 13 px controls. Each frame is the real panel size (820×480 default; 560×280 minimum) over a desktop backdrop. Tabs, segmented controls, theme rows, and sliders are clickable.

Full-panel page

in-place navigation, no modal at all
ReusesThe panel itself becomes the editor — header swaps to a back button + title; the content area hosts the tab tree. Nothing overlays anything.
Why it worksMaximum room for controls (the panel resizes to 560–∞ px), zero scrim so the entire UI restyles live as you pick themes. Feels like iOS/macOS settings navigation.

Bottom sheet

popover-native, thumb-friendly
ReusesScrim + menu surface like the dialog, but anchored to the panel's bottom edge with a grab handle — reads as "the panel opens up".
Why it worksKeeps the toolbar visible (title context stays "AI Usage"), slides down to dismiss, partial-height states possible (half = quick theme switch, full = all controls).
Watch outBottom sheets are an iOS idiom — charming in a menu-bar popover but less macOS-conventional than a centered dialog. Needs drag physics to feel right.

Settings-menu drill-in

quick switcher inside the ⌘K popover
ReusesThe existing SettingsPopover page stack (like Switch Layout → Columns). "Appearance…" pushes a submenu page: mode + theme list.
Why it worksZero new surfaces — the 90% case (switch theme / mode) is two clicks from ⌘K. "Open VSX…" and "More Controls…" escalate to a dialog (options 1–4) for the rest.
Watch outNot a full replacement — sliders, fonts, presets, and marketplace don't fit a 320 px menu. Best used alongside one of the other options, not instead of.
